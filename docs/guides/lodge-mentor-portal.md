# Lodge Mentor portal (future release)

*The portal is built and tested but closed. It opens for a Lodge Mentor only when Provincial leadership activates it for them or their Lodge.*

Once activated, **My Lodge** appears at the top of the Academy. You will see only the Lodges where you are the current Lodge Mentor.

- **Overview**: your Lodge's mentoring profile and arrangements. Update them directly if your activation allows, or **propose a change** to Lodge details for your Assistant Provincial Grand Mentor to accept.
- **New members**: mentoring information for newer members of your Lodge, including Personal Mentor allocation (Yes, No or Unknown).
- **Support**: ask the Provincial team for help (a visit, training, a speaker, guidance) and follow progress.
- **Activity**: contacts your Assistant has chosen to share with the Lodge.
- **Report**: a Lodge-level export, if activated.

Your Academy learning is the same whether or not the portal is open, and stays with you if your appointment ends. When you hand over to a new Lodge Mentor, portal access passes with the appointment.
