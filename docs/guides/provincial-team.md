# Provincial team guide

For the Provincial Grand Mentor, Deputy, Assistant Provincial Grand Mentors and team members. You see the parts of the Hub your role allows. Sign in with your password, then the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

*Mentoring is about people. The Hub is here to help you notice where a conversation might help, not to create paperwork.*

## Dashboard
Your starting page. Leadership see the whole Province:
- coverage of Lodge Mentors (in place, vacant, unconfirmed);
- open support requests and overdue follow-ups;
- recent new Initiates, learning, events and things awaiting approval.

Assistants see their own Lodges, tasks, visits and requests. Use **Customise** to hide or reorder panels.

## Lodges
- **Directory**: search and filter by area, mentoring status, Lodge Mentor (vacant or unconfirmed) and allocation. "My Lodges" shows your allocation. Save any filtered view; export to Excel, CSV or PDF if your role allows.
- **A Lodge page** has five tabs:
  - **Overview**: officers and the Lodge Mentor, with appointment history, mentoring arrangements, status and details.
  - **Activity**: a timeline of contacts, visits, reviews, tasks and status changes.
  - **Members**: degree dates and, for your Lodges, mentoring journeys, with gentle prompts where a conversation may be welcome.
  - **Notes**.
  - **Support**.
- **Log contact**: a line or two is enough. Tick "create a follow-up task" if you need reminding.
- **Plan visit**: optionally adds it to the Provincial calendar. Record the outcome afterwards.
- **Annual review**: five short questions. Leave anything blank that isn't useful.
- **Record appointment**: a new Lodge Mentor (or other officer). The previous holder's dates are kept.
- Assistants can view every Lodge but edit only their allocated Lodges.

Mentoring statuses describe where things stand operationally. They are never an assessment of a Lodge.

## Members
- Search by name or membership number; filter by Lodge, area, stage, initiation dates and Personal Mentor allocation.
- **Journey**: stage, Royal Arch (information, not recruitment), next review date.
- **Personal Mentor**: Yes, No or Unknown, with the date confirmed and who confirmed it. Naming the mentor is optional.
- **Local corrections**: if Hermes or Adelphi has a detail wrong, record the correction with a reason. Imports will not overwrite it.
- If a member asks to change their details from their account page, the request appears at the top of their record.

## Support
- **New request**: ordinary requests route automatically to the Lodge's Assistant.
- **A request page**: update status and priority, add updates (tick "visible to requester" to share one), add actions (they become tasks), escalate, then record the outcome and close.
- **Confidential concern**: use the separate route. Only the Provincial Grand Mentor and Deputy see it at first; they decide who handles it. Welfare, safeguarding and disciplinary matters are referred through the Province's existing procedures, and the Hub records where they were referred.

## Tasks and Team
- **Tasks**: My tasks, Overdue, Created by me. Reassigning notifies the new owner.
- **Team**: announcements and team notes.
- **Meetings**: agenda, attendance, minutes (draft, then approved), decisions, and actions that become tasks.

## Events and calendar
- Create events and training sessions with capacity, waiting list, joining details, trainers and resources. Paid events link to Octra-Lodge.
- **On the day**:
  - **Presenter view** shows a QR code attendees scan to check in.
  - **Scan** reads attendees' personal QR codes.
  - Manual changes need a reason.
- **Complete** the event to send follow-up and feedback requests.
- The calendar combines events, visits, meetings and your task dates. Subscribe to your personal feed from My Account.

## Communications
1. New campaign: subject, message (use `{{first_name}}`), category, audience.
2. **Audience**: filter by Lodge, area, office, role, stage, initiation dates, training or event participation; add or exclude individuals.
3. Check **Recipient preview**: it shows exactly who receives it and who is excluded, with the reason (preferences, bounced address, duplicate and so on).
4. **Send test to me**, then **Submit**. Province-wide messages need approval from the Provincial Grand Mentor or Deputy.
5. **Delivery** shows sent, delivered, bounced, clicks and unsubscribes. Opens are shown where measurable and are not proof of reading.

Replies go to the Provincial mentoring inbox.

## Library and knowledge
- Documents carry a reference, issue, owner, review date and access level. New issues go Draft → Review → Approved → Published; the previous issue becomes Superseded.
- Some documents ask readers to acknowledge the current issue.
- Knowledge articles: FAQs, guidance, good practice and examples. Examples must be anonymised before publishing.

## Reports
- Standard reports for Lodges, membership (including 12, 24 and 36-month cohort retention), learning, events and communications. Read the data limitations shown with each.
- The **Report builder** lets you choose a dataset, columns, grouping and chart; save, share and schedule it.
- **Quarterly report**: generate a frozen draft, edit the narrative, send to review. Leadership approve and publish, then download the PDF. An approved quarter never changes.

## Imports (leadership)
1. Imports → New: upload the Hermes, Adelphi or Portal report (CSV or Excel).
2. Choose or save a column mapping.
3. Review the tabs: New, Updated (with field changes), Unchanged, Duplicates, Conflicts, Invalid, Possible removals.
4. Resolve each pending row, then approve and commit.

Things to know:
- Mark whether the report is **partial** or **full**. A member missing from a report is never treated as having left. On a full report the Assistant gets a "check membership" task.
- New Initiates notify the Assistant, and nobody is emailed automatically.
