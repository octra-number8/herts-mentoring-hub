# Herts Mentoring Academy: a guide for learners

Welcome. The Academy is for Lodge Mentors, Personal Mentors and newer Brethren. Everything here is about your own learning: nobody else can see your progress except the Provincial learning team, and your reflections are private to you.

*Prepare · Guide · Support · Enlighten*

## Getting started
You will receive an invitation email. Open the link, choose a password (at least 12 characters) and you are in. Next time, sign in with your password, or choose "Email me a link" to sign in without one.

## My Learning
Your home page. **Continue learning** takes you straight back to where you left off. You will also see suggested courses (essential ones first), upcoming training, your certificates and achievements.

## Courses
Browse by pathway (Lodge Mentor, Personal Mentor, Newer Brother) or by topic. Open a course to see what it covers and how long it takes, then **Enrol**. Lessons can include:

- short readings and videos,
- scenarios where you choose what to do and see what happens,
- reflections: a few private sentences on your own experience,
- knowledge checks: if you don't pass first time, you'll see why and can try again.

Finish every lesson and you'll receive a certificate in **My Certificates**, ready to download.

## Training events
Book a place at workshops and evenings. If an event is full you join the waiting list and we'll email you if a place comes up. On the day, scan the QR code shown by the presenter, or show your own QR code from the event page. Afterwards you can leave feedback. Some events give a certificate of attendance.

## My Progress and My Plan
**My Progress** shows your courses, quiz results and training attendance. You can record learning done elsewhere, such as Solomon modules, and attach evidence if you wish. **My Plan** is your own development plan: what you'd like to learn next and by when.

## Resources
Approved guides and articles from the Province, plus links to Solomon (UGLE's learning platform).

## Your account
Change your password, choose which emails you receive (essential messages about your account always arrive), subscribe to your training calendar, and ask for corrections to your contact details. The account page also explains what the Hub holds about you.
