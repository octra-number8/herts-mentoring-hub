# ADR 0004: Hertfordshire canon takes precedence over the build brief's wording

Status: accepted, 25 October 2026

## Context
The build brief lists the principles as Prepare, Support, Guide, Enlighten and uses titles such as "Provincial Mentor". The library canon (A1 Principles Issue 1a, A3 Style, Terminology and Data Guide) fixes Prepare, Guide, Support, Enlighten, and the titles Provincial Grand Mentor, Deputy Provincial Grand Mentor and Assistant Provincial Grand Mentor, and bans "PGM", "onboarding" and em-dashes in member-facing text.

## Decision
The Hub follows the canon. A3 also names Hermes as UGLE's membership system; imports support Hermes, Adelphi and Portal report formats.

## Consequences
Wording matches every other Hertfordshire mentoring publication. If the brief's ordering was intended, the change is made in A1 first and then in the Hub.
